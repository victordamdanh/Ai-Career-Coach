Campus Operations Data Analyst Intern
Fictional internship for classroom practice

Help the campus operations team identify service bottlenecks and explain findings to student-services staff.

Required qualifications
- Clean spreadsheet data and summarize trends using Excel pivot tables.
- Write SQL queries that join tables and aggregate results.
- Present findings and a recommendation to a nontechnical audience.

Preferred qualification
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard with filters for location and time period.
