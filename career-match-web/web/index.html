Suggestion sources

Reported job titles come from the U.S. Department of Labor’s O*NET 31.0 Database under CC BY 4.0, with titles trimmed, deduplicated, and supplemented. U.S. place names come from the 2025 Census Gazetteer.

    How job-fit scoring works

    Required qualifications count twice as much as preferred qualifications. A match earns full credit, partial evidence earns half credit, and a gap earns zero.

    The score estimates qualification alignment. Check eligibility and application requirements in the official posting.
