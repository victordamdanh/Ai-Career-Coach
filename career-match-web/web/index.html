      How scoring works

      The resume score uses five categories worth 20 points each. The job-fit score gives required qualifications twice the weight of preferred qualifications. A match earns full credit, partial evidence earns half, and a gap earns zero.

      • Organization 20 pts
      • Clarity 20 pts
      • Impact 20 pts
      • Relevance 20 pts
      • Skills 20 pts

      The score estimates qualification alignment. Review employer-specific eligibility and application questions in the official posting.
